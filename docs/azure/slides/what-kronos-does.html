Kronos  ·  a foundation model for the language of financial markets

What Kronos does

In one line: Kronos reads recent price candles and writes the next ones. It's an open-source AI model pre-trained on candlestick data from 45+ exchanges — built to treat markets like a language and forecast what comes next.

How it works

Markets as a language, in three steps

Kronos borrows the idea behind large language models. A sentence is a sequence of words; a chart is a sequence of candles. Kronos learns the "grammar" of price action, then continues the sentence.

Step 1

Tokenize the candles

A specialized tokenizer turns each continuous candle (OHLCV) into a small set of discrete tokens — like turning a sound into letters, so a language model can read it.

KronosTokenizer · BSQuantizer
Hierarchical tokens: a coarse "s1" token + a fine "s2" token per step.
Step 2

Predict the next tokens

A GPT-style decoder-only Transformer reads the token sequence (plus time-of-day / day-of-week context) and predicts the next token — again and again, one step at a time.

Kronos · autoregressive
This is "autoregressive": each new candle feeds the guess for the one after.
Step 3

Decode back to prices

The predicted tokens are decoded back into candles and un-normalized to real price levels — giving you forecasted open/high/low/close (and volume) for each future step.

KronosPredictor.predict()
From raw history to forecast in one call — normalization handled for you.
Two ideas that make it useful
Probabilistic, not a single line

It samples many possible futures

Because it predicts a distribution over next tokens, you can draw many candidate paths and average them — tuned with temperature, top-p and sample_count. More samples → a steadier, less jumpy forecast.

Foundation model

Pre-trained broadly, adaptable to you

It's trained on data from 45+ global exchanges, so it works out of the box — and can be fine-tuned on your own instruments and timeframes to sharpen it for your task.

Straight talk

What it's good for — and what it isn't

Good for

  • Short-horizon forecasts of price & volume candles
  • Momentum / direction signals over a chosen window
  • A base to fine-tune on your own market data
  • Feeding a real-time monitoring & alerting loop

Not this

  • Predicting unscheduled shocks — no model can
  • A guaranteed, profitable trading strategy
  • Financial advice — forecasts are inputs, not decisions
  • A replacement for your risk controls & human judgment
